*Stay on camera — this is YOUR conviction. Small hand gestures fine.*

> "If I'm selling my camera to a stranger, one of us has to risk everything. Middlemen solve it — for a fee, holding both sides' money, by their own rules. Crypto fixed custody with smart contracts — but brought seed phrases, jargon, and, for disputes, judgment nobody can inspect."

### BEAT 3 — WHAT I BUILT · 0:45–1:10 (~50 words)

*CUTAWAY B1 (passkey) as you say "fingerprint"; back to camera for "So I built…"*

> "So I built **EscrowLens**. It's passkey-native — you sign in with a fingerprint; no seed phrase, ever. One audited contract holds the funds. And when there's a dispute, an AI arbiter reads the on-chain facts and each side's statement, then signs a recommendation — cryptographically bound to that one escrow."

### BEAT 4 — THE THESIS · 1:10–1:35 (~45 words)

*CUTAWAY B2 (signature card) then B3 ("cannot move funds") — let the audience READ it for a beat.*

---

### BEAT 5 — WHY IT'S REAL + CLOSE · 1:35–1:55 (~50 words)

*Back to camera. Final line straight into the lens; hold 2s before stopping recording.*

> "I built the whole thing for real: every escrow lifecycle path is settled on Monad testnet with verifiable transactions, and the arbiter is registered on-chain as an ERC-8004 agent. Monad's instant, near-zero-cost settlement is what makes evidence-grade escrow practical. **EscrowLens — trust you can verify, not promises you have to believe.**"
